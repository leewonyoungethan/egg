    Egg Heist

    You're a fox with a coop and big ambitions. Three rivals live on the same farm.

    1. Buy eggs off the hatchery belt and carry them home. Each egg in your coop earns coins every second.
    2. Sneak into a rival's den and steal their eggs. If the owner tags you, the egg goes back.
    3. Rivals raid you too. Tag a thief to win your egg back, or lock your coop for 20 seconds.
    4. Each stage asks you to steal a set number of eggs before the clock runs out. Every stage gives you less time and puts more, faster guard dogs on the dens.
