Egg Heist

You're a fox with an empty pen. North of your farm lie four zones full of eggs, and every zone is guarded.

  1. Run north, grab an egg with E, and carry it back to your pen. Keep moving: guards catch foxes who dawdle.
  2. Jump with Space to hop over guards, mud and snap traps.
  3. Eggs hatch into pets. Pets earn coins every second. Rare eggs can hatch big, golden or rainbow pets.
  4. Train on the treadmill to run faster. Each zone further north has more guards, faster guards and rarer eggs.
